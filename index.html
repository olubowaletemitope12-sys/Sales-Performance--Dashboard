Sales Performance Dashboard

Microsoft Excel

Findings

  1. Furniture generated the highest revenue, contributing approximately ₦4.85 million, making it the strongest-performing product category.
  2. The East region recorded the highest total revenue, at approximately ₦4.11 million, while the West recorded the lowest at approximately ₦1.72 million.
  3. Most orders were completed successfully, with 512 completed orders out of 599 total orders, while only 28 were cancelled and 11 were marked as cancelled. This indicates a relatively high order-completion rate.

Recommendations

  • Increase focus on Furniture, since it generated the highest revenue.
  • Investigate the West region's lower performance and identify ways to improve sales there.
  • Maintain and improve order completion while investigating the small number of cancelled orders to understand why they occurred.
