Sales Performance Dashboard

Sales Analysis using Microsoft Excel and Power BI

Project Overview

This project analyzes sales performance using a Superstore dataset. The analysis focuses on sales, profit, products, regions, customer segments and shipping modes.

Key Findings

  • Technology recorded the highest sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.
  • Technology generated the highest profit.
  • The West region recorded the highest sales and profit.
  • The Consumer segment generated the highest sales and profit.
  • Copiers, Phones and Accessories were among the top profit-generating products.
  • Standard Class recorded the highest sales among shipping modes.
  • Faster shipping did not necessarily result in higher sales.

Recommendations

  • Focus on high-performing Technology products while monitoring profitability.
  • Develop strategies to improve sales and profitability in weaker regions.
  • Continue targeting the Consumer segment while identifying opportunities in other segments.
  • Review shipping strategies based on customer needs and profitability rather than speed alone.
  • Use product-level performance data to identify products with strong profit potential.
